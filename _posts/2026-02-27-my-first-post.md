Some things resist compression. A genome variant that upends a diagnosis. A mountain you weren't expecting. The feeling of landing somewhere new and not knowing what the next hour holds.

I work with data for a living — sequencing reads, variant calls, population-scale cohorts. Most of what I do disappears into reports and pipelines. A blog is the opposite of that: imprecise, unhurried, and stubbornly human.

## What this will be

I've been writing on Medium for a while, but I wanted a place that felt more like mine. So here it is.

Expect two flavors of posts:

**Technical.** Things I've learned the hard way about working with genomic data — bioinformatics workflows, tools I've found useful, ideas about where the field is heading. I'll try to write for people who are curious but not necessarily specialists.

**Personal.** Travel, snowboarding, the occasional video project. Less polished, more honest.

## A note on pace

I'm not committing to a schedule. I'd rather write something worth reading once a month than fill up a feed with noise. Quality over cadence.
